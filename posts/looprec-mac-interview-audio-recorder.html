如果你需要在公司、共享办公空间或宿舍参加面试，又没有一间可以外放声音的独立会议室，LoopRec 提供了一种更适合 Mac 用户的复盘方式：戴耳机参加面试，同时录制 Mac 播放的系统声音与自己的麦克风输入。

LoopRec 官方产品页：looprec.tosky.dev

English: A Mac Audio Recorder for Interview Review

After an online interview, it is easy to remember the overall feeling but forget the exact questions, follow-ups, and wording that shaped the conversation. LoopRec is a lightweight native macOS app for recording system audio and microphone input at the same time.

It is designed for people who need a practical interview recording workflow in a shared office, bedroom, or any space where playing the interview through speakers is not appropriate. Put on your headphones, join the interview as usual, and save the conversation locally as an M4A file for later review or transcription.

Download LoopRec from the Mac App Store

Recording notice: Always follow the law and workplace policy where you are. Tell other participants and obtain consent when required before recording an interview or meeting.

为什么面试复盘需要同时录两路声音？

单独录麦克风，只能留下自己的声音；单独录系统音频，又缺少自己的回答。对面试复盘来说，更有价值的是保留完整的对话上下文：

  • 面试官的问题与连续追问；
  • 自己回答时的停顿、表达和技术细节；
  • 问题与回答之间的节奏；
  • 面试结束后可以回听、整理和转写的完整音频。

LoopRec 支持系统声音、麦克风，或两路同时录制。它不是屏幕录制软件，不需要录制画面，也不需要之后再从视频中提取音轨。

戴耳机参加面试，也能录下对方声音

传统的“用手机放在 Mac 旁边录音”会带来环境噪声、回声和距离问题；录屏软件则往往会生成更大的视频文件。LoopRec 的使用思路更直接：

  1. 在 Mac 上打开 LoopRec；
  2. 选择系统声音和麦克风输入；
  3. 插入耳机并正常参加线上面试；
  4. 开始录制前确认两路音频电平；
  5. 面试结束后停止录音，得到本地 M4A 文件。

系统声音来自 Mac 正在播放的会议或应用音频，麦克风则保留你的发言。由于声音直接在本机处理，耳机不会让系统音频“消失”在录音里。

LoopRec 的核心特点

1. 系统声音 + 麦克风双段录音

你可以录制系统声音、麦克风，或者同时录制两者，并分别调整两路音量。实时电平显示有助于在开始前发现静音、输入设备选错等问题。

2. 原生 macOS 应用，体积小

LoopRec 使用 Swift 开发，安装包约 1.2 MB，面向 macOS 15 及更高版本。它专注于音频录制，不把不需要的视频画面、摄像头素材和复杂编辑功能带进面试复盘流程。

3. 直接得到本地 M4A

停止录音后，LoopRec 会将两路音频混合并保存为 M4A。文件保存在你选择的本地目录，不需要登录 LoopRec 账号，也不需要上传到第三方服务器。

4. 适合后续转写与复盘

M4A 文件可以直接导入飞书妙记等工具生成逐字稿，也可以使用你习惯的本地音频播放器、转写服务或笔记流程。建议复盘时重点标记：高频追问、没有回答完整的技术点、可以用项目证据补强的经历，以及下一次需要主动澄清的问题。

5. 免费开始，按需升级

LoopRec 免费版每次最多录制 40 分钟；如果需要更长的单次录音，可以选择一次性购买 LoopRec Pro 解除时长限制。具体价格和可用地区以 Mac App Store 页面显示为准。

English: Why LoopRec Works for Headphone Interviews

Many Mac recording tools are built around screen capture. That is useful for tutorials, but unnecessary when your goal is to review an interview conversation. LoopRec focuses on the audio path:

  • capture Mac system audio;
  • capture your microphone;
  • mix both sources into one M4A file;
  • keep the recording on your Mac;
  • import the file into a transcription workflow later.

This makes LoopRec useful beyond job interviews: remote meetings, research calls, user interviews, online lessons, and personal voice review. The key use case is simple: record a conversation clearly without relying on speakers or a phone placed next to your laptop.

A simple interview review workflow

A recording is only useful when it becomes a review habit. After the interview, you can use this four-step loop:

  1. Listen once without pausing. Recover the structure and the interviewer’s priorities.
  2. Mark exact questions. Separate the opening question from later follow-ups.
  3. Compare question and answer. Note where the answer was concrete, where it drifted, and where evidence was missing.
  4. Write a short improvement list. Turn recurring gaps into preparation topics for the next interview.

The goal is not to record everything forever. It is to replace unreliable memory with a searchable, reviewable record—while respecting everyone’s consent and applicable recording rules.

隐私、权限与使用边界

LoopRec 官方说明：录音文件只保存在用户选择的本地文件夹，不上传录音，不包含广告或分析 SDK。应用需要的系统权限，仅用于在本机完成用户主动发起的录音。

但“本地保存”不等于可以在任何场景下无条件录音。美国各州、欧洲不同国家以及公司内部政策，对通话和会议录音的告知、同意与保存可能有不同要求。正式面试前，请确认：

  • 面试官和招聘方是否允许录音；
  • 是否需要在开始前明确告知并获得同意；
  • 录音文件应该保存多久、由谁访问；
  • 是否包含个人数据、商业秘密或其他受保护信息。

常见搜索问题：Mac 面试录音与系统声音录制

How do I record system audio and microphone on a Mac?

Use a Mac audio recorder that can capture both system audio and microphone input. LoopRec is built for this workflow on macOS 15 or later and saves the mixed recording as a local M4A file.

Can I record an interview while wearing headphones?

Yes, when the recording app captures the Mac’s system audio rather than relying on a room microphone. LoopRec is designed to record the system audio played through the Mac and your microphone input while you use headphones.

What is the best way to record an online interview for review?

First check consent and company policy. If recording is permitted, use a dedicated audio recorder instead of a full screen recorder when you only need the conversation. Test both audio levels before the interview and keep the resulting file in a controlled local folder.

Can I use LoopRec to create a transcript?

LoopRec creates a local M4A recording. You can then import that file into Feishu Minutes or another transcription tool. Transcription accuracy depends on the audio, speakers, language, and service you choose.

Does LoopRec upload recordings?

The official product page states that recordings remain in the local folder selected by the user and are not uploaded to LoopRec’s server. Review the current privacy policy and App Store disclosures before using it for sensitive conversations.

适合哪些人？

LoopRec 特别适合以下场景：

  • 在开放办公区或共享空间参加面试；
  • 不方便使用扬声器，但希望保留面试官声音；
  • 需要回听线上技术面、HR 面或项目沟通；
  • 想把录音导入转写工具，而不是保存完整视频；
  • 希望使用轻量、原生、无账号依赖的 Mac 工具。

如果你需要摄像头画面、屏幕标注、剪辑和直播布局，LoopRec 并不替代专业录屏软件；如果目标是清楚保存一段允许录制的对话，它的范围反而更集中。
